<p>An activity for exercise routines. The teacher sets a routine as part of the course, and each student does it on a phone or a computer, following a 3D figure that moves at the tempo, on the side and for the sets the routine says. Every session is saved in Moodle, counts towards completion and grade, and the teacher follows each student week by week. For physical education classes and for schools that train physiotherapists, coaches or sports scientists.</p>
<h3>How it works</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>The teacher builds the routine</strong> in the settings of the activity: “Build the routine” opens the builder, with the exercises and their sets, repetitions, tempo and side, the rest between sets, or the rounds of a circuit, the days a week and the acceptable discomfort. The routine comes back to the form by itself.</li>
<li><strong>Each student follows the figure</strong>: it goes up, holds and comes down at the pace of each exercise, on the right side, set after set, with a sound and a vibration on every repetition and a voice that says the phases and counts. At the end the student notes down what was done, the discomfort, whether an exercise was hard or could not be done, and a note.</li>
<li><strong>Every session reaches the course</strong> as soon as it ends; without a connection it waits on the phone and goes as soon as there is one.</li>
<li><strong>The follow-up</strong> shows who has done what and what there is to look at: days above the acceptable discomfort, exercises found hard or not done, notes. The file of each student shows the discomfort week by week and every set done against the one asked for.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Discomfort with a method</h3>
<p>Each routine has an acceptable level of discomfort (5 out of 10 unless the teacher sets another), after the pain-monitoring model (Thomeé, 1997). The days above it are flagged, and the morning after a session the student is asked how the area felt on getting up.</p>
<h3>Completion and grade</h3>
<p>The completion rule “Do the expected sessions”. With a grade in points, the grade follows the share of the expected sessions done; the teacher can always grade by hand. Teachers can try the routine as a student would, and nothing they do is saved.</p>
<h3>In the Moodle app</h3>
<p>The activity works in the official Moodle app with nothing to set up: its page shows the routine and the progress, “Start” opens the player inside the app with the student signed in, and the progress updates as soon as the player closes.</p>
<h3>The exercises</h3>
<p>The plugin comes with twelve exercises for the whole body, in two figures: sit to stand, glute bridge, shoulder external rotation with a band, half squat, heel raises, single-leg balance, knee plank, opposite arm and leg on all fours, cat-camel, clamshell, seated knee extension and knee to chest.</p>
<h3>Privacy</h3>
<p>Covered by the Privacy API (export and deletion), with backup and restore of the sessions and course reset. Everything stays in your Moodle: no external services, no CDN, no tracking.</p>
<h3>Languages</h3>
<p>The pages of the activity in English and Spanish, other languages through AMOS. The player, its exercises and its recorded voice in English and Spanish, following each user's language in Moodle.</p>
<h3>Requirements</h3>
<ul>
<li>Moodle 4.5 to 5.3.</li>
<li>Tested on Moodle 4.5, 5.3 and the development branch, with PHPUnit in CI.</li>
<li>Any theme, in light and dark, and any modern browser.</li>
</ul>
